The engineer's residual-risk note was right to flag the hero case. Confirmed
on **both** the live-competition hero (Süper Lig/Europa League) and the
World Cup **archive** hero — it's a property of the Hero component's mobile
layout generally, not one competition. The last two lines of the hero body
paragraph run directly under the gold/green FAB stack; a real user landing
on the football home page on a phone sees "...across the books and de-vigged
to the r[eal]" and "...league table. E[very]" with word-endings hidden
behind the icons. Reproduced 3 ways: immediate screenshot, screenshot after
a 1.2s settle, and after scrolling away and back up to the top (the last one
matters because the existing scroll-up-reveals-the-stack behavior puts it
right back over the hero every time a mobile visitor returns to the top).

**Root cause:** the stack's only reveal gate was a timer (900ms after
mount) plus "reveal on scroll-up or once scrolling stops" — neither checks
*where* on the page the visitor actually is. On this hero, the mobile layout
runs close enough to a full viewport height that the fixed FAB zone and the
hero's last paragraph lines occupy the same screen coordinates for the
entire time the visitor is looking at the top of the page.

**Fixed** (file ownership: `src/components/site/useHideOnScroll.ts`, the one
file that needed a change): added a floor gate — on viewports under 640px,
every reveal path (the initial timer, scroll-up, idle-settle) now stays
hidden while `window.scrollY <= 140`, instead of revealing unconditionally.
Once the visitor has actually scrolled past that zone, the existing
hide-while-scrolling-down / reveal-on-up-or-idle behavior applies exactly as
before. Viewports at or above 640px are completely untouched — confirmed
desktop's own hero has full clearance already (screenshot
`t1-home-desktop-settled.png`), so gating it there would only cost
discoverability for no benefit. `npx tsc --noEmit -p .` and
`npx eslint src/components/site/useHideOnScroll.ts` both clean.

**Not yet re-verified against a running build.** A local `next dev` start to
visually confirm the fix was blocked by this session's own permission
classifier ("Modify Shared Resources" / "Interfere With Workloads") —
respected rather than worked around. The fix is code-reviewed and
type/lint-clean but needs one more look (mobile home hero, at rest, no
scroll) right after this lands on `main` and redeploys, before this item is
called fully closed.
